# Calculate age from date of birth and date of record

Calculate age from date of birth and date of record

## Usage

``` r
calculate_age(record_date, dobyr, dobmnth, dobday)
```

## Arguments

- record_date:

  Date data collected (in format "%Y-%m-%d %H:%M:%S" or "%d/%m/%Y
  %H:%M")

- dobyr:

  Year of birth

- dobmnth:

  Month of birth

- dobday:

  Day of birth

## Value

An integer vector of ages
